# 恋爱大师聊天宝典提炼

This reference distills the provided `恋爱宝典(Y)(1).pdf` into operational guidance. Use it to produce stage-aware reply options and predictions, not to quote the source verbatim.

## Three Stages

### 1. 刚加上的女生

Goal: start a low-pressure chat, not make her like the user instantly.

Usable openings:

- Simple greeting: `哈喽`, `Hi`, `你好呀`.
- Light emoji/sticker opening if the platform and user's style fit.
- If her moments/friend-circle has visible recent content, open from a recent detail instead of interrogating.
- Ask name or nickname only when natural and not as a dry interview.

Default reply patterns:

- Plain and safe: `哈喽，终于加上你了。`
- Light/playful: `哈喽，新朋友上线，先打个招呼。`
- Context-based: `刚看你朋友圈好像很会找好吃的，我先怀疑你是隐藏美食雷达。`

Avoid: long self-introduction, checking household-style questions, heavy flirting with no rapport, needy compliments.

### 2. 聊了几次的女生

Goal: restart the topic by offering value, context, or curiosity.

Restart channels:

- Share yourself: work/life/funny moments/current state, preferably with a detail that shows taste, discipline, warmth, or humor.
- Use her recent moments/friend-circle: food, travel, pets, afternoon tea, work, photos, shows.
- Talk about topics she likely enjoys: food, travel, pets, zodiac, entertainment, gossip, shows, psychology tests.
- Ask for advice in an area she may know: clubs, makeup, gifts, local food, photos, outfits, travel tips.
- Trigger curiosity: say you noticed a feature, had a funny thought, or want to ask a small question.
- Use a sticker or meme when text would feel too abrupt.

Default restart patterns:

- Self-share: `刚从健身房出来，手臂有点不听使唤了，感觉今晚筷子都得双手举。`
- Moments-based: `看你最近那张照片，感觉你很会找周末放松的地方。`
- Curiosity: `我发现你有个挺明显的特点。`
- Advice: `想请教你一个女生视角的问题。`

Avoid: `在吗`, repeated probing, one-question-after-another interviews, apologizing for interrupting, explaining too much.

### 3. 对自己有好感或暧昧的女生

Goal: gently raise emotional tension while reading feedback carefully.

Principle: treat her feedback like traffic rules. If she warms up, continue. If she hesitates, slow down. If she resists, change topic or stop.

Usable styles:

- Mild tease: respond to her short/cute answer with playful exaggeration.
- Soft role claim: hint at closeness jokingly, without making pressure.
- Invite a small imagined scene: movie, food, walk, coffee, shared errand.
- Calibrated flirt: only when her previous feedback is clearly warm.

Default patterns:

- `告诉你一个小秘密。`
- `你这回复有点敷衍，手机是不是只剩一个字的电量了。`
- `我发现你越来越会让我想找你聊天了。`
- `这话听起来像是在给我递台阶，那我可就顺着下来了。`

Avoid: explicit sexual pressure, possessive claims, over-speed escalation, ignoring discomfort.

## Chat Principles

### Frame

Maintain equal footing. Do not over-send long paragraphs after short replies. Do not chase when she has not replied. Show principles, self-respect, and a life outside the chat.

### Attitude

When she asks testing questions such as `你跟每个女生都这么聊吗`, `你喜欢我什么`, or `你谈过几个`, answer with calm playfulness and a grounded attitude. The attitude matters more than a perfect factual answer.

### Rhythm

Match her reply length, speed, and energy. If she sends a short neutral reply, do not send a speech. If she sends warm details, expand and ask a natural follow-up.

### Perception

Infer the emotional subtext before replying. Look for warmth, curiosity, joking, resistance, fatigue, boredom, or boundary-setting.

### Emotion

Good romantic chat creates light emotional movement: surprise, amusement, curiosity, warmth, nostalgia, anticipation. Avoid dry logic and interview mode.

### Cold Reading

Use gentle, broad observations to create resonance, not to manipulate. Keep it light and reversible:

- `你看着挺外向的，但应该不是谁都能真的走近你。`
- `感觉你是那种表面很好说话，其实心里有自己标准的人。`

## Topic Bank

Use these topics to continue after extracting keywords from her reply.

- Food: tastes, restaurants, cooking, desserts, late-night snacks, "what food saves your mood".
- Travel: favorite cities, scenery, local food, funny travel stories, next destination.
- Pets: cats/dogs, pet personalities, funny videos, names, daily chaos.
- Zodiac: playful personality hooks; use only lightly and avoid sounding superstitious or judgmental.
- Photography: photos, angles, outfits, scenery, how she chose a picture.
- Shows/movies: current shows, comfort shows, movie invitations when rapport is already good.
- Work/study: positive, confident, and energetic framing. Do not dump negative emotions.
- Stories: interesting past experiences, embarrassing moments, lessons, unexpected events.
- Experiences: childhood, school, first job, moving cities, hobbies, moments that shaped her.
- Future plans: cities, dreams, lifestyle wishes, things she wants to try.
- Reading/learning: books, useful ideas, fun facts, personal growth.

## Keeping Topics Flowing

When she replies, use this sequence:

1. Pick keywords from her message.
2. Choose one keyword with emotional or story potential.
3. Expand with comment + self-disclosure + light question.

Example structure:

`评价/共情 + 自己的小经历 + 开放式轻问题`

If she says `刚吃完饭，躺沙发上看电视`, keywords are `吃饭`, `沙发`, `看电视`. Possible expansions:

- Food: `这状态很舒服。我现在最纠结的是附近外卖都吃腻了，你今天吃的是自己做的还是外卖？`
- Feeling: `吃完饭瘫沙发看电视，属于一天里最像奖励自己的时刻了。你在看什么？`

## Techniques

### 推拉

Use a tiny tease followed by warmth. Fit it to the relationship stage.

Pattern: `轻轻推开/调侃 + 拉回来/肯定`

Example: `你这张照片有点危险，容易让人误会你很会骗夸奖。`

### 幽默风趣

Use exaggeration, mock seriousness, or playful self-positioning. Keep it short.

Example: `恭喜你，微信好友列表又新增一位还算靠谱的人。`

### 角色扮演

Borrow a small role from the chat scene, such as teacher/student, boss/employee, interviewer/candidate, detective/suspect, food critic/chef.

Example: `你这个夸奖我收下了，幼儿园小红花奖杯差点又亮起来。`

### 勾起好奇

Create an open loop, then close it quickly. Do not drag it out too long.

Pattern: `我发现你有个特点 / 我突然想到一件事 / 我有个问题想问你`

### 曲解

Playfully reinterpret her words in a harmless alternate meaning.

Example: if she says `我们又不熟`, reply: `叮，刚去微波炉转了一圈，现在熟一点了。`

## Mistakes To Avoid

- 跪舔讨好: over-concern, over-helping, no boundaries.
- 暴露需求感: showing that her reply controls the user's mood.
- 自嗨: ignoring cold feedback and talking alone.
- 过早表白: confession is a result of attraction, not a shortcut to create it.
- 急色: sexualizing too fast or pressuring her.
- 邋遢: poor profile, careless language, crude presentation.
- 太直: literal interrogation, no emotion, no playfulness.

## Feedback Calibration

Warm signals:

- She asks questions back, explains details, laughs, uses playful language, sends longer replies, shares personal stories.
- Response strategy: expand, tease lightly, or move toward a shared scene.

Neutral signals:

- She answers but does not add much.
- Response strategy: pick one keyword, add a little self-disclosure, ask one easy question.

Cold signals:

- `嗯`, `哦`, `哈哈`, emoji only, long delay with no content, repeated one-word answers.
- Response strategy: reduce investment, avoid chasing, send one light low-pressure message or pause.

Boundary signals:

- She says no, uncomfortable, not interested, stop, too much, weird, or changes topic away from flirtation.
- Response strategy: acknowledge and slow down. Do not push.

Testing signals:

- Questions like `你是不是对谁都这样`, `你是不是很会撩`, `你喜欢我什么`.
- Response strategy: respond with calm humor plus a grounded point; do not beg, defend intensely, or over-prove.

## Three-Option Generation Rules

For every user request, produce exactly three distinct options:

1. Safe/stable option: low pressure, broadly usable.
2. Playful/emotional option: humor, curiosity, or light tease.
3. Progressive option: slightly moves the relationship forward, only as far as her feedback allows.

For each option, include:

- The exact reply text.
- The most likely warm/neutral/cold response from her.
- A short follow-up direction.
- A quick note when the option should be avoided.
